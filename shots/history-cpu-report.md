## Performance changes

Private news groups now have reverse indexes for affected people, close kin, villages and hexes. Learning and promotion maintain those indexes. Queries inspect only the listener's declared audience buckets, retaining the newest relevant unheard story in each original group and the original ranking/tie rules. Immutable story metadata is reused during promotion.

Courtship candidate pools are assembled once per village per seasonal boundary from the existing eligible resident buckets. Ancestry is shared for the owned boundary, and seeded order hashes are computed once per person. Status is rechecked after intervening weddings. Caller-controlled queries do not retain ancestry caches across edits.
