# Village resource customs

Common woodland follows actual settlement land use. `commonLand(state)` starts at each household plot, house and shared place. It follows the walkable graph for two hex steps. The closest settlement seed wins an overlap. Village ID breaks equal-distance ties. Household plot tiles and explicitly owned trees stay private. Gold lines show the visible common boundary when the player inspects a common tree. Unseen ground stays hidden. Distant unclaimed trees remain free.

`resourcePermission(state, actorId, targetId)` returns `kind`, `permitted`, and the actual `villageId` or `ownerId`. Private access uses existing `householdAccess`. Oaths grant no private store, crop, item or building permission. The actual chopping task checks private permission again. Building clearance destroys standing trees through its existing privileged operation. It creates no player incident or logs. History generation creates no incident.

Only actual player chopping can create an incident. An adult resident of the claiming village must be outside and within 300 world units of both the player and tree. The observer must have a live planner. Initial observers remain the eligible witness pool. Busy observers queue the warning until free. Urgent needs, assigned work, crops, cargo, friendship help and travel take priority. The witness uses normal navigation, terrain, field and fence routing. Moving targets change the route. Death, departure, hunger and tiredness release the visit or dialogue. An inaccessible target creates no completed contact or penalty.

At speech reach, the witness stops every pending forbidden common chopping order. The encounter holds both speakers until acknowledgement or refusal. Pause, speed and other UI controls remain available. Both responses stop cutting. Both record the completed exchange through `beginInteraction` and `completeConversation`. Arrival can introduce the speaker. Starting an approach cannot record completed conversation contact. Observed cutting before the first warning shares one incident. New cutting requires an explicit order.

The first completed warning costs 3 goodwill from witness to offender. A new offence after a completed warning costs 6. The witness reports to the current elder through navigation and five minutes of actual nearby exchange. The elder then loses 2 goodwill towards the offender, or 4 for a repeat. If the elder is the witness, the witness loss applies once. Nobody receives a spouse or bystander penalty. Events use explicit audiences. Failure to reach an elder does not create a report or conversation. The elder is the oldest eligible resident adult in a real local household, excluding playable people. Ties use person ID. A living eligible assigned `elderId` remains valid. Death or departure selects the next eligible person.

A met adult can request a real need from their actual household through their person panel. The accepted request names that adult as requester. Existing favour rewards restore the same directional goodwill. The warning and report remain factual events. A harvest or fuel request must be accepted and physically completed under the existing favour system. Chat gives the existing 2 goodwill. Fuel and harvest rewards keep their existing quantities. No reputation reset exists.

An independent adult can discuss an oath with the elder after a completed conversation and at least 10 goodwill towards that person. A camp or independent household qualifies. A household attached to another village must first settle independently. Membership grants common gathering in that village, with ordinary mutual aid and social ties as obligations. Private permissions stay as before. There are no taxes or military duties. The elder explains the complete rule before a separate acceptance button. The footer then shows Village member. Declining grants nothing.

Oaths live in `ecs.village[personId].memberships[villageId]`, with day and receiving elder. They travel with the person through the existing identity bundle. They do not pass to an heir. Actual residents in a local household receive household recognition. A temporary guest does not. An explicit leave record overrides household recognition and ends common gathering rights. Leaving needs a conversation with the current elder. Old help, warnings and reports remain true.

For independent browser acceptance, start seed 13 with `?intro=skip`. Finish character selection, then run `await require('./scripts/customs-fixture').customsFixture(page)` from the Playwright driver. The labelled fixture places the visitor by a real common tree and an actual local adult on an adjoining walkable tile. It grants no goodwill, knowledge, membership, favour completion or resource reward. The return value names the tree, candidate observer, elder and village. Natural availability can select another actual observer. The fixture also records initial person positions. Inspect the tree, choose Cut down, then confirm Cut without permission. Fast speed reaches a physical warning. Acknowledge and observe the witness reporting. Use real chats and requests in each person's panel to repair standing. The elder's panel shows the oath threshold and rights. Complete the oath visit and accept before cutting common woodland with permission. Discuss leaving to remove rights. Inspect a private tree/store to confirm that oath rights never grant access.

The warning is social, without confiscation or force. An unwitnessed offence causes no penalty. A permanently unavailable witness or elder leaves an unresolved incident, without a blocking dialogue. Commons are local customs, not noble land titles. Household recognition follows actual residence rather than ancestry. Mutual aid is expressed through the existing favour and social systems. It does not create a separate quota or tax counter.

Final player-facing warning: “Please leave our woodland alone.” The witness says: “These trees belong to our village. You need our leave to cut them. Speak to our elder if you want to settle here.” The replies are “Fair enough. I’ll stop.” and “I don’t agree.” Both stop cutting. The line above the replies gives exact witness and elder goodwill losses. Tree inspection says that gold lines mark the commons, private property stays private, and unclaimed woodland is free. Algorithms remain in this document and the development API.

General mechanical and storyboard drivers now select permitted timber for their ordinary building scenarios. They keep their original physical delivery, quantity and outcome assertions. The independent customs scenario uses protected timber and the explicit confirmation instead.
