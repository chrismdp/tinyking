# Reading people's lives

Relationship copy describes one person's opinion of another. Public is an acquaintance; Social is a familiar acquaintance; Personal is a friend; Intimate is one of their closest friends. Intimate alone says nothing about romance. Gossip stores the speaker's disclosed ring at that time. It does not inspect the other direction or silently refresh the report.

`knowledge[viewer].people[person].conversation` records the day and both positions at a completed conversation. Only successful timed Chat, Ask about or Gift tasks, and completed local NPC conversations, record it. Selection, introductions, learned names, gossip about somebody, seasonal summary conversations and tree navigation do not. `feelingHint` requires this actor's contact and suppresses present impressions of dead people. The relationship meter also waits for contact. Other information keeps its existing trust rules.

`completeConversation` discloses events already known to the speaker. Familiar acquaintances can learn family history and tools; friends can also learn private quarrels, reconciliations, gifts, courtship and keepsakes. `people[person].possessions` records disclosed item IDs. The person sheet no longer lists undisclosed possessions. Event knowledge remains immutable and deduplicated by event ID.

`biography` selects at most two supported, distinct facts. It uses learned events, visible kinship and disclosed possession IDs. A carried inheritance requires current ownership, actual custody, the last transfer's previous owner, and a learned matching inheritance event/date/item. It never infers who raised a child. Parent loss requires a known parent link and a learned death. Remote relatives need no local position. Dead people cannot claim to still carry something. With insufficient evidence the sheet invites an introduction or a family conversation.

`currentActivity` supplies the person sheet, legacy selection and overhead bubbles. Normal mode omits generic idle, walking and waiting labels. It preserves work, directed travel, social partners, queued order purposes, material waits and need interruptions. Debug adds the current primitive, or says no task. The accessible Debug button beside Pause changes only UI state and requests redraws; it cannot advance the clock or needs.

The ordered player uses survival and explicit task planning, with a stationary wait at the end. Cancelling the last order also stops its food-seeking walk. They do not enter autonomous farm, haul, visit, gathering-space or home relocation routines. Without a running or ready explicit order, food and sleep use actual carried or reachable supplies and beds without moving. Food reach is ten world units; resting beside a bed permits fifty. If those are unavailable, needs keep rising and the task panel offers Find food or Rest. These are explicit orders which use the existing physical self-care routines. During other explicit work, automatic food/sleep trips still interrupt and resume the task. This user-priority rule replaces the older design assumption of unrestricted automatic player food trips. NPC self-care remains unchanged.

Children keep the existing ageing, appearance, kinship and survival rules. Under 18s skip adult jobs, trades, field ploughing and heavy hauling. Children aged six or more can make short crop-care visits to an actual sown household garden or field. Fifteen minutes of work advances one crop's growth by three game hours, once per slot/day. Available care runs between play episodes. It creates no goods. Existing growth, harvest, seed consumption and household ownership still apply. Generated vegetable gardens use two real crop slots and the existing grain crop/goods system. Available older children get priority for light care; adults cover gardens otherwise.

Children aged four or more can meet a household member or known companion for a half-hour chase. Both walk to surveyed, reachable village-green spaces, then alternate positions every five game minutes. Each episode must fit the actual terrain travel time plus a half-hour of play into the two-hour arrival/expiry and daylight window. Available children can start further completed episodes throughout free time. Needs, death, adulthood, absence and attention can interrupt. Play never pairs children with adults.

Adult visits reuse local navigation, relationships, avoidance and household motives. They can start from nine in the morning through the evening, only during real downtime, including a walk to an ambient gathering place. Accepted work, field claims, friend help, journeys and needs keep priority. Conversation requires proximity, both participants' patience and consent. Ordinary visits contain up to three half-hour conversations; mutually closest partners may stay for five, still within actual patience and need limits. A visit is recorded only after its first completed conversation, allowing interrupted approaches to retry without duplicate news. Seasonal marriage rules are unchanged.
